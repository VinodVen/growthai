Most small business software lets you export customers as a CSV file. Follow these steps:

  1. Go to Square Dashboard → Customers
  2. Click Import/Export → Export Customers
  3. Download the CSV file
  4. Come back here and upload using Upload CSV/Excel tab
Square exports include: name, email, phone number

  1. Go to Clover Dashboard → Customers
  2. Click Export button (top right)
  3. Download CSV
  4. Upload here using Upload CSV/Excel tab
Clover exports include: name, email, phone

  1. Go to Toast Web → Guests
  2. Click Export Guest List
  3. Download CSV
  4. Upload here using Upload CSV/Excel tab
Toast exports include: name, email, phone

  1. Go to Mindbody → Clients
  2. Click Export → Export to CSV
  3. Download and upload here

  1. Go to QuickBooks → Customers
  2. Click Export → Export to Excel
  3. Save as CSV or upload the Excel file directly

  1. Go to contacts.google.com
  2. Click Export → Google CSV
  3. Upload here
