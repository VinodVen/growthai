Specialty Occupation Analysis — 8 CFR 214.2(h)(4)(ii)
Specialty Occupation Justification

Under 8 CFR 214.2(h)(4)(ii), a "specialty occupation" means an occupation that requires the theoretical and practical application of a body of highly specialized knowledge, and attainment of a bachelor's or higher degree in the specific specialty (or its equivalent) as a minimum for entry into the occupation in the United States. The position of Full-Stack Software Engineer / AI Platform Developer at Revvio satisfies each of the four alternative criteria below.

Criterion 1: Bachelor's Degree is the Normal Minimum Requirement

The Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) Occupational Outlook Handbook for SOC 15-1252 (Software Developers) states: "Software developers typically need a bachelor's degree in computer science or a related field." This is universally recognized across the software industry. Every comparable company hiring engineers to build AI-integrated SaaS platforms requires at minimum a bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a directly related discipline.

The Revvio platform's engineering work — including multi-tenant architecture design, AI API integration, OAuth2 security implementation, and PostgreSQL database engineering — cannot be performed by individuals without the theoretical foundation provided by a four-year degree in computer science or engineering.

Criterion 2: The Degree Requirement is Common in the Industry

Software engineering roles involving AI/ML integration, cloud infrastructure, and SaaS architecture universally require bachelor's degrees or higher across the industry. A review of comparable job postings from companies building similar AI-powered SaaS platforms (Mailchimp, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo) consistently lists a bachelor's degree in Computer Science or Software Engineering as the minimum requirement for equivalent roles. The skills required at Revvio are no less specialized than those at comparable organizations.

Criterion 3: Employer Normally Requires a Degree for this Position

InnovateCore LLC requires a minimum bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a directly related field for the position of Full-Stack Software Engineer working on the Revvio platform. The technical complexity of the Revvio platform — 134 API endpoints, 13 database models, 7 third-party API integrations, multi-tenant architecture, AI content generation engine, and cryptographic security implementation — makes it impossible to perform these duties without the theoretical foundation acquired through a four-year degree program.

Criterion 4: The Nature of Duties is So Specialized and Complex That Knowledge Requires a Degree

Conclusion: The position of Full-Stack Software Engineer / AI Platform Developer at Revvio clearly qualifies as a specialty occupation under 8 CFR 214.2(h)(4)(ii). Every duty performed requires the theoretical and practical application of a body of highly specialized knowledge in computer science and software engineering — knowledge that is normally acquired through a bachelor's or higher degree in the specific specialty.

Security Implementation Evidence
The Revvio platform implements production-grade security across all layers:
  • Bcrypt password hashing (adaptive cost factor) — prevents brute-force attacks
  • Constant-time password comparison — prevents timing side-channel attacks
  • HTTPS-only session cookies with HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax flags — prevents XSS session theft and CSRF
